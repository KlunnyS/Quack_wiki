## About Quack Wiki

Quack Wiki is a community knowledge base for the Quack game project. It collects gameplay notes, development updates, article references, and useful background information in one place.

## What We Are Building

- A readable wiki for players and contributors
- A place for approved articles, guides, and project notes
- A simple admin workflow for reviewing and managing content

## Project Goals

The goal is to keep information easy to find, easy to update, and safe to manage through roles, article statuses, and dashboard tools.
